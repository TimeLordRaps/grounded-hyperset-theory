# Governance

## Current phase

Grounded Hyperset Theory is founder-maintained mathematical and computational reference work.
Publication makes the source and formal calculi pathways transparent and inspectable; it does
not imply institutional accreditation.

## Project states

- **Implemented base:** Accessible Pointed Graphs, bisimulation equivalence, constructive membership, and canonical quotienting.
- **Pathways:** Abstraction & quotienting, meta-calculus, hyper-calculus, language calculus, and real analysis bridge.
- **Experimental:** Transfinite extensions and infinite-graph bisimulation approximations.

## Decision rights

TimeLordRaps is the project maintainer and release manager. Pull requests and issues are reviewed against
the mathematical proofs and empirical test suites.

The repository's Apache License 2.0 governs the code, specifications, and documentation.
